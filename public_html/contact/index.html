Contact FAQ

How do I contact Oncotics?

Send a message to Amit Suresh, Creator and Architect of Oncotics, on LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/amit-suresh.

Can I send patient details for advice?

No. Oncotics does not provide medical advice and cannot receive personal health information. Please do not send names, dates of birth, record numbers, reports or images about a patient.

Yes. Suggestions for new public sources, broken links, data discrepancies and accessibility issues are welcome via LinkedIn.
