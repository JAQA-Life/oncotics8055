FACT means a preserved source-reported field, not independent clinical confirmation. DERIVED means a documented transformation. ASSUMPTION is a researcher-stated hypothesis. SIMULATED covers every synthetic persona, action, graph and engine narrative.

These social simulations are research-only. They do not establish clinical efficacy, safety, patient outcomes, approval, eligibility or geographic change. Do not enter patient or confidential data. Private simulation still retrieves public evidence from external providers; cloud simulations send the compact seed, question and assumptions to configured external services after explicit consent.

Evidence is attributed on each source record. Full source and dependency notices, model licenses, deployment instructions, provenance rules and verification limits are included in the project source documentation.
